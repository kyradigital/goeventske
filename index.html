How it works

Four steps. No developer.

Set your event up weeks ahead, then let it sell while you get on with everything else.

Build your event

Name, date, venue, poster. Add as many ticket types as the event needs — early bird, regular, VIP table — each with its own price, quantity and sale window.

Share one link

Every event gets its own page. Put it in your bio, on the poster, in the group chat. Buyers pay by M-Pesa and their ticket is issued the moment the payment clears.

Scan on event day

Open the scanner on any phone and point it at the QR. Green means admit, amber means the ticket has already been used. Every scan is recorded against the guest.

Get paid

We hold the takings until 24 hours after the event finishes, then send your share to your M-Pesa number. The 10% is shown to you before you ever publish.

What you get

Everything the event needs, in one place

Ticketing, payment, entry and reporting — built to work together rather than bolted on.

A unique QR code on every ticket

No two tickets are alike. Each one carries its own long, random code that cannot be guessed or worked out from another. Scan it once and it is spent — a screenshot forwarded to a friend is turned away at the entrance, and the person holding it is told when and where it was used.

A ticket scanner built in

No extra hardware and no second app to buy. Your team opens the scanner on the phone already in their pocket and points the camera at the guest's QR. Admit or refuse in under a second, and it keeps working when the network at the venue does not.

Tickets emailed to every buyer

The moment M-Pesa confirms, the ticket lands in your customer's inbox with the QR code, the venue, the time and their booking reference. They can screenshot it, print it, or pull the email up at the entrance. Nothing for you to send by hand.

Analytics worth opening

Revenue by day, which ticket type is carrying the event, how many have actually come through the entrance against how many you sold, and what time of day people buy. Enough to price the next event properly instead of guessing.

Add your team, keep your keys

Invite the people working the event and give each one their own login. They can scan tickets and see the guest list; they cannot see your payouts, your M-Pesa number or your other events. Nobody has to share the one account that holds the money.

M-Pesa in, M-Pesa out

Buyers get an STK push and enter their PIN on their own handset — never on our site, never to a person. You do not need a Daraja account or a paybill of your own. Your share goes to your M-Pesa number 24 hours after the event, in one clean payment.

Questions

The things organisers ask first

When exactly do I get my money?

24 hours after your event's end time, sent to the M-Pesa number on your account. We hold it that long so that any disputed payment surfaces before the money leaves us, not after. You can see the exact figure, and the 10% taken, on the Payouts page from the moment the first ticket sells.

How do my guests actually pay?

M-Pesa. They open your event link, choose their tickets, enter their number, and their phone buzzes with the payment request. They enter their PIN on their own handset — never on our site, and never to a person. Their ticket appears on screen and arrives by email the moment it clears.

Can someone screenshot a ticket and send it to a friend?

They can send it. It will not work twice. Every ticket carries its own long random code, and the first scan spends it — whoever presents it second gets an amber screen showing when and where it was already used.

What happens if the internet drops at the venue?

The scanner keeps working. It already holds the list of valid tickets, so it can admit guests and record the scans, then sync when signal returns. The one thing it cannot do offline is catch a ticket scanned on a different phone in the same dead spot.

Can I let my team in without giving them everything?

Yes. Each person you invite gets their own login. They see the guest list and the scanner and nothing else — not your payouts, not your M-Pesa number, not the events you have not announced yet.

What if I cancel the event?

Refund everyone from the dashboard in one action, straight back to the M-Pesa numbers they paid from. You are not charged our 10% on a refunded ticket — Safaricom's own transaction fee is the only thing that does not come back.

How far ahead can I set an event up?

As far ahead as you like. Build it, save it as a draft, and publish when you are ready to announce. Tickets can go on sale months out, and you can add or close ticket types at any point without disturbing what has already sold.
